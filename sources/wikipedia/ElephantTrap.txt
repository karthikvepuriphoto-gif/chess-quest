In [[chess]], the '''Elephant Trap''' is a faulty attempt by [[White and Black in chess|White]] to win a [[pawn (chess)|pawn]] in a popular variation of the [[Queen's Gambit Declined]].

The earliest recorded occurrence of the trap seems to be the game [[Karl Mayet]]–[[Daniel Harrwitz]], Berlin 1848.<ref>[http://www.chessgames.com/perl/chessgame?gid=1242968 K. Mayet vs. D. Harrwitz, Berlin 1848] [[Chessgames.com]]</ref>
