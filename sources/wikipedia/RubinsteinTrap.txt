The '''Rubinstein Trap''' is a [[chess opening]] [[Chess trap|trap]] in the [[Queen's Gambit Declined|Queen's Gambit Declined, Orthodox Defense]].
Black loses a pawn after Nxd5 due to the threat of their [[queen (chess)|queen]] being trapped on the {{chessgloss|back rank}} by White's Bc7. 
{{AN chess|pos=toc}}

==History==
The trap takes its name from [[Akiba Rubinstein]], who had the misfortune of falling into it twice, in the games [[Max Euwe|Euwe]]&ndash;Rubinstein, [[Bad Kissingen]] 1928, and [[Alexander Alekhine|Alekhine]]&ndash;Rubinstein, [[San Remo 1930 chess tournament|San Remo 1930]]. Rubinstein was not the first to fall victim to the trap; the first recorded game featuring the trap is [[Amos Burn]]&ndash;[[Heinrich Wolf]], [[Ostend]] 1905.
